ReLeaf Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: July 10, 2026

RayLabz is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users of ReLeaf. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including any content or behaviour that exploits, endangers, or harms children.

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Any content, behaviour, communication, or account involved in child sexual abuse or exploitation will be removed immediately and appropriate action will be taken, including account suspension or permanent removal.

Prohibited Content

The following is strictly prohibited on ReLeaf:
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Any sexual content involving minors.
  • Grooming or attempting to establish inappropriate relationships with minors.
  • Soliciting sexual images or personal information from minors.
  • Sexual exploitation, trafficking, or abuse of children.
  • Any content encouraging or facilitating abuse of children.
  • Links to websites containing child exploitation material.

Reporting

Users may report inappropriate content or user accounts directly within the application or by contacting RayLabz using the email address below. All reports are reviewed promptly. Where required by applicable law, we will cooperate with relevant law enforcement authorities.

Enforcement

Violations of these standards may result in:
  • Immediate removal of offending content.
  • Temporary or permanent suspension of accounts.
  • Reporting to the appropriate authorities where legally required.

Industry Standards

ReLeaf supports internationally recognized child safety initiatives and expects all users to comply with applicable laws regarding child protection. Our policies align with guidance published by:
  • Google Play Developer Program Policies
  • The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
  • Applicable European Union and local child protection legislation

Contact

If you discover content that may violate these standards, please contact us immediately.
