Delete Your ReLeaf Account

This page explains how users of ReLeaf, developed by RayLabz, can request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to raylabzg@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request – ReLeaf.
  3. Include the email address associated with your ReLeaf account.
  4. If possible, include your username to help us locate your account.
  5. We may contact you to verify ownership of the account before processing your request.

What Information Will Be Deleted?

Once your request has been verified, we will permanently delete your ReLeaf account along with the personal information associated with it, including:

  • Your display name.
  • Your username.
  • Your email address.
  • Your user profile.
  • Your accumulated points.
  • Your streak history.
  • Your leaderboard records.
  • Your avatar and customization settings.
  • Your friendships and social connections within ReLeaf.
  • Any other personal data associated with your account stored in Firebase Firestore.

What Information May Be Retained?

We do not retain copies of your personal account information after deletion, except where retention is required by applicable law or where necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Protect against fraud or abuse.
  • Maintain platform security.

Any retained information will be kept only for the minimum period required by law and will then be permanently deleted.

Processing Time

Verified account deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days.
